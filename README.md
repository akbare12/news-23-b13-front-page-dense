# THE WORLD TODAY — Monday 23 November 2026

A dense one-page front page summing up Monday 23 November 2026 in 31 stories
across 6 regions: the AI rout in Asia (Kospi −3.3%), the UN chief's call to
regulate AI before it's too late, and the US lead over China at a record low
(Asia Power Index gap 5.3). Three hero scenes, ten illustrated cards, a world
map with numbered pins, an ALSO list, and a DATE CHECK box that keeps out
stories from other days.

`main.py` (standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` and verifies its size and sha256.
`draw.py` draws the image from scratch with Pillow.
